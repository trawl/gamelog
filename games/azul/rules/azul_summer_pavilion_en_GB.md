# Azul: Summer Pavilion — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Coloured-stars side of the board, 2–4 players.*

## Goal

Have the most points after **6 rounds** plus the final scoring. Everyone starts
on **5 points**. Ties are shared.

| End-of-game bonus | Points |
|---|:---:|
| Complete the multicoloured centre star | 12 |
| Complete the red star | 14 |
| Complete the blue star | 15 |
| Complete the yellow star | 16 |
| Complete the orange star | 17 |
| Complete the green star | 18 |
| Complete the purple star | 20 |
| Cover all the 1s | 4 |
| Cover all the 2s | 8 |
| Cover all the 3s | 12 |
| Cover all the 4s | 16 |

Also lose **1 point** for each unused tile left in the corner spaces of your
board after the final round.

## Setup

1. Factory displays: **5** for 2 players, **7** for 3, **9** for 4. Each gets 4
   tiles.
2. 132 tiles in the bag (6 colours). 10 tiles are laid on the supply spaces.
3. A round counter shows the round and the **wild colour**. Round 1 is purple.

## A round

### 1. Acquire tiles

On your turn take **all tiles of one non-wild colour** from a factory or from the
centre. Leftovers from a factory go to the centre.

- If the factory (or the centre) holds any **wild** tiles, you must also take
  **exactly one** of them.
- If only wild tiles are there, you may take **just one**.
- The first player to take from the centre takes the **start player token** and
  moves their marker back one space per tile taken that turn (including the wild
  one), not past space 1.

The phase ends when everything is empty. Keep your tiles beside your board.

### 2. Play and score tiles

Starting with the token holder, each turn you place **one** tile on an empty
diamond space (never on pillars, statues or windows). Or pass.

- A space needs **as many tiles of its colour as the number printed on it**.
  Place one on the board and discard the rest of those needed to the tower.
- Tiles beyond what you need stay beside your board for later turns.
- On the multicoloured centre star, any colour goes anywhere, but all 6 tiles
  must be **different colours**.
- **Wild colour:** wild tiles may stand in for missing tiles, but you must
  still hold at least one real tile of the colour you place. The wild tiles you
  use are discarded to the tower. Wild tiles never stand in for their own
  colour: used on their own, they go on the star of the wild colour.
- Wild tiles are wild **only in the round they were taken**.

**Scoring a tile:** 1 point for the tile, plus 1 point for each tile it is
connected to (spaces next to each other on the same star). Move your marker.

**Bonus tiles** taken from the supply when you finish surrounding:

| Surrounded | You take |
|---|---|
| A pillar (its 4 spaces) | 1 tile of your choice |
| A statue (its 4 spaces) | 2 tiles of your choice |
| A window (its 2 spaces) | 3 tiles of your choice |

Refill the supply from the bag (or the tower) before the next turn.

**Passing:** you may keep up to **4** tiles by putting them on the corner spaces.
They score nothing. Discard the rest to the tower and lose **1 point per tile
discarded**. You take no more turns this round.

### 3. Next round

The start player moves the counter on, refills factories with 4 tiles each
(refilling the bag from the tower if needed) and puts the token back in the
centre. Everyone moves their corner tiles back next to their board.

## Gamelog tips

- Enter each player's round score, which can be negative from passing and marker
  penalties (down to -20). Totals start on 5 and are kept at 0 or above: if a
  negative round would take the total lower, the app records just enough of the
  penalty to bring it to 0.
- The star and number bonus buttons are only available in round 6, and each can
  be ticked once. The app adds their points for you.
- The game ends automatically after the sixth round, and ties are shared.
- Penalties (marker, passing, corner tiles) are not separate controls: include
  them in the round score you enter.
