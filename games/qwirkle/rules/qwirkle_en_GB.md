# Qwirkle — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Base game, 2–4 players.*

## Goal

Have the most points when the game ends. There are 108 tiles: 6 colours and 6
shapes, **3 copies of every colour-shape combination**.

| Scoring | Points |
|---|:---:|
| Each tile in each line you create or add to | 1 per tile |
| Completing a line of 6 (a **Qwirkle**) | +6 bonus (12 for the line) |
| First player to run out of tiles after the bag is empty | +6 |

## Setup

1. All tiles go in the bag. Each player draws **6** tiles and keeps them hidden.
2. Each player announces their longest set of tiles sharing one colour or one
   shape, **without duplicates**. The player with the biggest set plays it to
   start (a tie goes to the oldest tied player). The tiles go in one line and
   score as one line.
3. Play passes clockwise.

## A turn

Do **one** of these:

1. **Play** one or more tiles, score them, then draw back up to 6.
2. **Swap** any number of tiles instead: set them aside, draw the same number of
   new ones, then put the old ones back in the bag. You score **nothing**, and
   you cannot swap more tiles than the bag holds.

## Placing tiles

- All tiles you play in a turn must be in **one line** (same row or same column)
  and share **one attribute**: all the same colour, or all the same shape.
- They do **not** have to touch each other, but at least one must touch a tile
  already on the table, and the new tile must match it by colour or shape.
- Every line formed on the table, in any direction, must be all one colour or
  all one shape, with **no duplicate** tiles (only one blue square in a line of
  squares, for example).
- A line is **never longer than 6**.
- You may add to **both ends** of a line in one turn.
- Some spaces end up with no legal tile at all. That is normal.

## Scoring a play

- Count every line your play creates or extends: 1 point per tile in the line,
  **including tiles already there**.
- A tile that belongs to two lines (across and down) counts in both.
- **Qwirkle:** a completed line of 6 scores its 6 tiles plus 6 bonus points. You
  can complete more than one in a turn and score each.

Example: placing one tile that makes a line of 4 across and a line of 3 down
scores 4 + 3 = 7.

## End of the game

When the bag is empty, play goes on but nobody draws. The first player to use all
their tiles ends the game and gets the **6-point bonus**. The highest score wins.

## Common disputes

- **Duplicates:** two identical tiles (same colour and shape) can never be in the
  same line, even far apart.
- **Gaps:** a line only counts tiles that are touching in a row. Tiles with a gap
  between them are separate lines.
- **Two attributes in one turn:** you may not play a red square and then a blue
  circle in the same turn. They must share colour or shape.
- **Swapping scores 0:** and ends your turn.

## Gamelog tips

- Enter the score of each play. The Qwirkle bonus button records how many
  Qwirkles that play completed (up to 6) so they show in the statistics. The
  points still come from the score you type in.
- The game is finished explicitly from the app, since the end depends on the
  bag running out and a player emptying their hand.
- **App tie-break** (not part of the official rules): if players tie on score,
  the most Qwirkles wins, then the best single play. If still tied, they share
  the win.
