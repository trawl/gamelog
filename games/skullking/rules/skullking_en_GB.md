# Skull King — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Base game plus the optional rules the app supports, 2–8
players.*

## Goal

Over ten rounds, predict exactly how many tricks you will win. Exact bids earn
points, wrong bids lose points. **Highest total after the last round wins.**

## Setup

- Remove the blank cards, Loot, Kraken and White Whale unless you play the
  advanced rules.
- Round 1 deals 1 card each, round 2 deals 2, and so on up to 10 in round 10.
  With 8 players, deal 8 cards in rounds 9 and 10.
- The player left of the dealer leads the first trick, and the deal moves left
  each round.

## Bidding

Everyone chooses their bid in secret, then all reveal together with the fingers
of one hand (a fist is zero). There is no restriction on the total of the bids.

## The cards

- **Suits:** green, yellow and purple are ordinary suits numbered 1–14. Black is
  the **trump** suit and beats all other suits.
- Follow the led suit if you can. You may always play a special card instead,
  and you may play anything if you have no card of the led suit.
- **Escape (5):** always loses. If everybody plays one, the first played wins.
- **Pirate (5):** beats all suits including trump. Of several Pirates the first
  played wins.
- **Skull King:** beats all suits and Pirates, and loses only to a Mermaid.
- **Mermaid (2):** beats all suits and the Skull King, and loses to Pirates. If
  a Pirate, the Skull King and a Mermaid are all in one trick, **the Mermaid
  wins**, whatever the order.
- **Tigress:** declare when you play her whether she is a Pirate or an Escape.
- Leading an Escape (or Tigress as Escape): the next suited card sets the suit.
  Leading a Pirate, Mermaid or the Skull King sets no suit.

## Scoring

| Bid | Result | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 or more | Exactly right | +20 per trick won |
| 1 or more | Wrong | -10 per trick over or under |
| 0 | Won no tricks | +10 x cards dealt that round |
| 0 | Won 1+ tricks | -10 x cards dealt that round |

Example: bid 3, win 3 = +60. Bid 2, win 4 = -20.

## Bonus points

Bonus points count **only if your bid was exactly right**.

| Bonus | Points |
|---|:---:|
| Capture a green, yellow or purple 14 | 10 each |
| Capture the black 14 | 20 |
| Pirate captures a Mermaid | 20 each |
| Skull King captures a Pirate | 30 each |
| Mermaid captures the Skull King | 40 |

The bonus goes to the player who wins the trick, even if the card was played by
an opponent. Order of play inside the trick does not matter. The capturing card
must have won the trick: if a Mermaid takes a trick containing the Skull King
and a Pirate, only the Mermaid bonus is scored.

## Ties

Official rule: if the top score is tied after the last round, play more rounds
until someone is ahead.

## Optional rules

**Round lengths** (cards dealt per round; all selectable in the app on the
board before round 1):

| Mode | Cards per round |
|---|---|
| Standard | 1 to 10, ten rounds |
| Even Keeled | 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 |
| Skip to the Brawl | 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 |
| Swift-n-Salty Skirmish | five rounds of 5 |
| Broadside Barrage | ten rounds of 10 |
| Whirlpool | 9, 7, 5, 3, 1 |

**Advanced cards** (not modelled by the app beyond the prompts described below)
- **Kraken:** the trick is destroyed, nobody wins it, and the player who would
  have won it leads next.
- **White Whale:** special cards in the trick count as Escapes, numbers lose
  their suit, and the highest number wins (first played if tied). If only
  special cards were played, the trick is discarded.
- **Loot:** played like an Escape. The player who played it and the player who
  wins the trick are allies, and each gets **+20** if both bid exactly right.
- **Rascal of Roatan** (a Pirate ability): when you win a trick with that
  Pirate you may bet 10 or 20 on your bid: you gain it if you hit your bid and
  lose it if you miss.

**Rascal's scoring** (alternative scoring): every player's potential in a round
is **10 x cards dealt**. An exact bid earns all of it, a miss by exactly 1
earns half, a miss by 2 or more earns nothing. Bonus points follow the same
all, half or none pattern.

## Gamelog tips

- For every player enter the bid, the tricks won and any bonuses. The tricks
  won by everybody must add up to the cards dealt. If they are short by one or
  two the app asks whether the Kraken (and White Whale) discarded tricks.
- There are three **scoring modes**, chosen before round 1:
  - **Classic:** exact and wrong bids as above, with only the Skull King and
    Pirate bonus buttons.
  - **Standard:** adds Mermaid, Loot, Fourteens, Black Fourteen and Roatan
    buttons. At most 3 Fourteen bonuses and 4 Loot bonuses can be given in a
    round. Classic is not offered above 6 players.
  - **Rascal:** the Rascal scoring above, plus a **Cannonball** switch, an
    all-or-nothing choice worth 15 x cards dealt for an exact bid and nothing
    for any miss.
- Bonus buttons only unlock when a player's bid is exact. Loot needs an exact
  bid; Roatan unlocks when the player won at least one trick.
- The **zero-bid** and **Rascal** values use the **round number** in the app,
  not the cards dealt. These are the same in the Standard round sequence only.
  In other round lengths, adjust by hand or check the totals.
- The winner is the highest total after the last round. If it is tied, the app
  shares the victory instead of playing extra rounds.
