# Scrabble — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Standard English game, 2–4 players, based on the Mattel
rule sheet. Challenge handling differs between publishers and tournaments.*

## Goal

Have the highest score at the end by building interlocking words crossword
style. There are 100 tiles: 98 letters and **2 blanks** worth 0 points.

## Setup

1. Each player draws **7** tiles for their rack. Play goes clockwise.
2. Agree beforehand which **dictionary** settles disputes.

## First word

- At least **2 letters**, across or down (never diagonal), with one tile on the
  **centre star**.
- The centre star is a **double-word** square, so the first word is doubled.

## A turn

Do **one** of these:

1. **Play** tiles, then draw back up to 7.
2. **Exchange** some or all of your tiles: discard them face down, draw the same
   number, then mix the discards back into the bag. You score nothing.
3. **Pass** and score nothing.

## Placing tiles

- All tiles you place must be in **one row or one column**.
- Your play must connect with tiles already on the board and form words of 2 or
  more letters.
- Any tiles that end up touching others form words too, and **every such word
  must be valid**.
- You can: add letters to one or both ends of a word, play a word crossing an
  existing one at right angles using one of its letters, play a word alongside
  existing words (all the touching letters must make valid words), or bridge
  letters between two words.
- Tiles cannot be moved once played, except after a successful challenge.
- Words allowed: anything in the agreed dictionary, except words that need an
  initial capital, abbreviations, prefixes and suffixes on their own, and words
  with apostrophes or hyphens.

## Scoring

Add the face values of the letters of **every word you formed or extended** in
the turn.

| Square | Effect |
|---|---|
| Double letter (light blue) | Letter on it counts x2 |
| Triple letter (dark blue) | Letter on it counts x3 |
| Double word (light red, and the centre star) | Whole word x2 |
| Triple word (dark red) | Whole word x3 |

- Premium squares count **only on the turn a tile is placed** on them. Later
  words through them score face value.
- If a word covers two word squares, multiply together: **x4** (double and
  double) or **x9** (triple and triple).
- A tile in two new words counts in each word, with its premium in both.
- A **blank** scores 0 as a letter, but a blank on a word square still doubles
  or triples the word. You must say which letter it is, and it stays that letter.
- **Bingo:** using all 7 tiles in one turn adds **50 points**, after any word
  multiplier.

## Challenges

After a word is played and **before** the score is counted, any player may
challenge it and check the dictionary. If the word is not acceptable, the player
takes back their tiles and **loses their turn** (this is the Mattel rule; other
rule sets differ).

## End of the game

The game ends when:

- all tiles are drawn and a player uses their last tile, or
- no more plays are possible, or
- all players pass twice in a row.

Then every player **subtracts** the value of the tiles left on their rack. If a
player used all their tiles, they **add** the total value of everyone else's
leftover tiles.

## Gamelog tips

- Enter the score of each turn. The bonus buttons (double letter, triple letter,
  double word, triple word, bingo) record which bonuses the turn used so they
  show in the statistics. The points still come from the score you type in.
- **App tie-break** (not part of the rule sheet): if players tie on score, the
  one who used more bonuses wins, then the best single turn. If still tied, they
  share the win.
- There is no separate control for the leftover-tile adjustments at the end.
