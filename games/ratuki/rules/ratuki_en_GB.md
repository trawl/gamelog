# Ratuki — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule sheet. Base game, 2–5 players.*

## Goal

Score **100 points** or more over several rounds. Each round everybody plays at
the same time, with no turns.

## Cards

Each player has their own coloured deck. Cards are numbered **1–5**. Each deck
also holds 2 **RATUKI** cards, which count as any number you like.

## Setup of a round

1. Each player shuffles their own deck and places it face down as their **draw
   pile**.
2. Take 3 cards from it into your hand.
3. Keep three separate piles in front of you: **draw**, **junk** and **score**.
4. Someone shouts "RATUKI!" and everybody starts at once.

## Playing

- You may start a centre stack only with a **1** or a **RATUKI** card.
- The number of centre stacks can never exceed the **number of players**.
- To add to a stack, play a card that is **one higher or one lower** than the
  top card. The design does not matter, but you may not put the same number on
  top of itself. RATUKI cards count as whatever you need.
- Stacks are won by getting a **5** (or a RATUKI card) on top: you grab it and
  put those cards face up on your **score pile**, shouting "RATUKI!".
  You can also build a stack downwards to make a bigger pile to win.
- Always refill your hand to 3 cards from your draw pile after playing.
- Stuck with a card? Put it face down on your **junk pile** and take another.
- **One hand only:** you may lay cards with one hand. It does not matter which,
  but once you have chosen, you keep to it.
- The round ends the moment a player runs out of cards and shouts "OUT!". Everyone
  stops immediately.

## Round scoring

For each player:

> **Round score = cards in your score pile, minus cards in your draw pile, minus
> cards in your junk pile.**

Each card is 1 point. A round score can therefore be **negative**. Cards that
are still in your hand are not mentioned in the official sheet; agree this at
the table before playing.

Shuffle each colour back together and play the next round.

## End of the game

The first player to reach **100 points or more** wins.

- If several players pass 100 in the same round, the one with the **highest**
  total wins.
- If those players have exactly the **same** total, it is a **draw**.

A "speedy game" is a single round. The player with the most points in it wins.

## Gamelog tips

- Home rules: the app only records round scores, not cards or stacks. Enter
  every player's round score (using the formula above) at the end of each
  round. A single entry accepts -100 to 100.
- You can optionally click a player's box (or press Space in their score field)
  to mark them as the round's winner. The badge is just a label in the round
  table.
- The target score defaults to **100** and can be changed until the second
  round starts. When any player reaches it, the app ends the game and gives it to
  the highest total. Players tied on the highest total share the win, matching
  the official draw rule.
- A speedy game is simply a single round: note the winner without relying on
  the target.
