# Catan — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Base game, 3–4 players (5–6 with the extension).*

## Goal

Be the first player to reach **10 victory points (VP)** on your own turn.

| Source | VP |
|---|:---:|
| Settlement | 1 |
| City | 2 |
| Longest Road (5+ roads) | 2 |
| Largest Army (3+ knights played) | 2 |
| Victory Point card | 1 each |

## Setup

1. Each player places **2 settlements and 2 roads**. Settlements go on
   intersections, roads on the edges next to them.
2. Placing goes round in turn order, then **back in reverse order**. The last
   player therefore places twice in a row.
3. Every settlement must obey the **distance rule**: no settlement within one
   edge of another, whoever owns it.
4. Your **second** settlement collects one resource from each adjacent hex.

## A turn

1. **Roll** two dice. Every settlement on a hex with that number collects 1
   resource (cities collect 2). On a **7**, see *The robber*.
2. **Trade** and **build** in any order, as often as you like.
3. Optionally play **one** development card (it can be played before the roll).
4. Pass the dice.

If the bank runs out of a resource and several players would collect it: if
only one player is owed it, they get what is left. If more than one is owed it,
nobody gets that resource that roll.

## Building costs

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Road | 1 brick + 1 lumber | Must connect to your own road, settlement or city |
| Settlement | 1 brick + 1 lumber + 1 wool + 1 grain | Must connect to your own road; distance rule applies |
| City | 3 ore + 2 grain | Replaces one of your settlements |
| Development card | 1 ore + 1 wool + 1 grain | Drawn blind from the deck |

Piece limits: **15 roads, 5 settlements, 4 cities.** A city returns its
settlement to your supply, so you can build another one.

## Trading

- **With players:** on your turn only, any deal you both agree on. Players
  whose turn it is not may not trade with each other.
- **With the bank:** 4 of one resource for 1 of any other.
- **Harbours:** a settlement or city on a harbour improves the bank rate. A
  generic harbour (3:1) trades 3 identical resources for 1. A specific harbour
  (2:1) trades 2 of that resource for 1 of any other.

## The robber

When a **7** is rolled, nobody collects resources, and then in order:

1. Every player holding **more than 7 cards** discards **half** of them,
   rounded down.
2. The player who rolled **moves the robber** to any other hex.
3. They **steal 1 random card** from a player with a settlement or city on that
   hex.

The robber blocks the hex it sits on: it produces nothing until it is moved.
Playing a Knight moves the robber in the same way, steps 2 and 3.

## Development cards

You may play **one per turn**. You may not play a card on the turn you bought
it, except a Victory Point card, which can be revealed as soon as it gets you
to 10 VP.

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Knight** | Move the robber and steal 1 card. Counts towards Largest Army. |
| **Road Building** | Place 2 roads for free. |
| **Year of Plenty** | Take any 2 resources from the bank. |
| **Monopoly** | Name a resource: every other player gives you all of theirs. |
| **Victory Point** | 1 VP, kept hidden until you win. |

## Longest Road and Largest Army

- **Longest Road:** a single continuous road of **at least 5** segments. A
  fork counts only along its longest branch. An opponent's settlement or city
  on an intersection **breaks** the road there. Another player takes the card
  by building a road that is **strictly longer**.
- **Largest Army:** **at least 3** Knight cards played. Another player takes
  the card by playing **more** Knights than the holder.

**Ties:** the holder keeps a card while tied for the lead. If a broken road
leaves the holder behind and several other players are tied for longest, nobody
holds Longest Road until one of them is strictly ahead.

## Gamelog tips

- Track settlements, cities, Longest Road, Largest Army and VP cards on the
  scoreboard to see everyone's VP at a glance.
- Hidden VP cards only count once revealed, so remember to enter them when a
  player reaches 10 VP.
