# Parchís — quick reference

*A summary of commonly played Spanish Parchís rules for settling disputes at
the table. There is no single official rule book: editions and regions differ,
so agree on the variants below before you start. This sheet follows what the
app tracks (tokens in the goal and captures). 2–4 players (up to 6 on some
boards).*

## Goal

Be the first player to get **all 4 of your tokens into the goal**. Others may
keep playing for second and third place.

## Setup

- Each player has 4 tokens, all starting in their **home**.
- You need a **5** to bring a token out onto your own **start square**. Leaving
  home is compulsory when you roll a 5 and can do it.
- Tokens travel around the whole board, then up the **home column** of their
  colour into the goal.

## A turn

1. Roll the die (**one-die** edition) or both dice (**two-dice** edition,
   where you may add them for one token or use each die on a different token).
2. Move a token the rolled number of squares.
3. A **6** (one die) or a **double** (two dice) gives an **extra roll**.
4. Pass the dice, unless an extra roll or a bonus applies (see below).

**Three in a row:** rolling a third consecutive 6 (one die) or double (two
dice) sends the **last token you moved back home**, except if that token
has already reached the home column.

## Safe squares

- Squares marked with a circle, and the **start squares**, are **safe**: a
  token on them cannot be captured.
- Tokens of different colours can share a safe square.

## Barriers

- **Two tokens of the same colour on one square** form a **barrier**. No token,
  not even the owner's, may jump over or land past it, and it cannot be
  captured.
- If you roll a 6 (one die) or a double (two dice) and can move a token of your
  own barrier, you generally **must** open it.
- A start square holding two of your own tokens means you cannot bring out
  another one.

## Captures ("kills") and bonuses

| Event | Result |
|---|---|
| Land exactly on an opponent's token on an unsafe square | It goes **home**; you **count 20** squares with one of your tokens |
| A token enters the **goal** | You **count 10** squares with another of your tokens |

- A bonus is taken in full with **one** token, and is **lost** if no token can
  use it (some tables let the player pass instead).
- The goal needs an **exact** roll. A roll that is too high cannot be used by
  that token. Tables differ on whether it bounces back.
- Once **all** your tokens are out of home, a rolled 6 is counted as 7 (one-die edition).
- If an opponent's token sits on **your** start square and you roll a 5, it is
  captured so that you can come out. If two of their tokens are there, tables
  differ on which one goes home.

## Gamelog tips

- Each entry is one scoring event for one player: pick the player, set the
  **number of goals** (0 to 4) and, for each opponent, the **kills** made
  against them. An entry with no goals and no kills is rejected.
- The match ends as soon as a player's goals total reaches **4**.
- Kills are tallied in a killer-versus-victim table. Capturing your own token is
  recorded too (shown as a suicide).
- A **combo** is any action beyond the first in a single entry. Goals and kills
  both count, so one goal plus two kills is 2 combos.
- The 20-square and 10-square bonuses are not tracked, only goals and kills.
