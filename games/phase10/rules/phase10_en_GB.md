# Phase 10 — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Standard edition, 2–6 players.*

## Goal

Be the first player to **complete all 10 phases**. Each hand you try to lay
down your current phase. Whoever goes out first wins the hand, and everybody
else is penalised for the cards left in their hand. **Lowest total score
breaks ties.**

## The ten phases

| Phase | Requirement |
|:---:|---|
| 1 | 2 sets of 3 |
| 2 | 1 set of 3 + 1 run of 4 |
| 3 | 1 set of 4 + 1 run of 4 |
| 4 | 1 run of 7 |
| 5 | 1 run of 8 |
| 6 | 1 run of 9 |
| 7 | 2 sets of 4 |
| 8 | 7 cards of one colour |
| 9 | 1 set of 5 + 1 set of 2 |
| 10 | 1 set of 5 + 1 set of 3 |

- A **set** is cards with the same number, any colours.
- A **run** is cards in consecutive numbers (1 to 12), any colours. Runs do not
  wrap round from 12 to 1.
- **Phases must be completed in order, 1 to 10.** If you fail to complete your
  phase in a hand, you repeat it in the next one.

## Deck and setup

- 108 cards: numbers 1–12 in four colours (two of each per colour), **8 Wild**
  cards and **4 Skip** cards.
- Deal **10 cards** each. Turn the top card of the rest to start the discard
  pile. The player to the dealer's left goes first, play is clockwise, and the
  deal passes to the left after each hand.

## A turn

1. **Draw** the top card of the draw pile or of the discard pile.
2. If you hold your whole phase, you may **lay it down**, face up. You may
   never lay down part of a phase.
3. After your phase is down you may **hit**: add cards to any laid-down phase,
   yours or another player's, as long as they fit (a card to a set, the next or
   previous number to a run).
4. **Discard** one card to end your turn.

Only **one phase** can be laid down per hand. You may lay down extra cards with
it only if they extend what you are laying (extra cards of a set or run you
already have), never an extra set or run.

You get **credit for the phase the moment you lay it down**, even if somebody
else goes out first.

## Wild and Skip cards

- A **Wild** stands in for any number and any colour. You may use as many as
  you like, but a phase needs **at least one natural card**. Once placed, a
  Wild stays as that card until the hand ends.
- A **Skip** is discarded in front of a player of your choice: their next turn
  is lost. It counts as your discard.
- A Skip cannot be picked up from the discard pile, cannot be part of a phase
  and cannot be played on someone who is already skipped.
- If the first card of the discard pile is a Skip, the first player misses a
  turn.

## Going out and scoring

The first player to get rid of every card (by discarding or hitting) **wins the
hand and scores 0**. Everybody else scores the cards still in their hand:

| Card | Points |
|---|:---:|
| 1 to 9 | 5 |
| 10 to 12 | 10 |
| Skip | 15 |
| Wild | 20 |

Cards already laid down do not count. Players who completed their phase advance
to the next one even if they did not go out.

## End of the game

The game ends at the end of the hand in which someone completes phase 10. If
several players complete it in the same hand, the **lowest total score** wins.
Under the official rules a remaining tie is settled by those players playing
one more hand of phase 10: the first to go out wins.

## Gamelog tips

- Each round, record for every player the phase they aimed at and whether they
  completed it, plus their penalty points. The phase list on the side of the
  board shows who has cleared what.
- Scores are entered in multiples of 5. A player who did **not** complete their
  phase cannot score under 50, since they still hold all 10 cards.
- The **Phases in order** switch is on by default and can only be changed in
  the first round. Switching to **Free phase order** lets players choose any
  phase they have not cleared yet, which is not part of the standard rules.
- A match is won by the player who has cleared all 10 phases with the lowest
  total. If totals tie, the app prefers the lower score in the last round, and
  if that is also tied the victory is shared. This differs from the official
  rule, which is to replay phase 10.
