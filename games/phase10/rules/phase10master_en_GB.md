# Phase 10 Master — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
rule book in your box. Phase 10 Master edition, 2–6 players. The game flow is
the same as standard Phase 10; the phases are harder.*

## Which "Master" is this?

The app's Master edition uses the phase list of the **Ravensburger Phase 10
Master** (2008). It is not the same as the English "Phase 10 Masters Edition"
tin, whose rules (choose your phase each hand, set aside one "saved" card) are
different. 

## Goal

Be the first player to **complete all 10 phases**. Lowest total score breaks
ties.

## The ten phases

| Phase | Requirement |
|:---:|---|
| 1 | 4 sets of 2 |
| 2 | 6 cards of one colour |
| 3 | 1 set of 4 + 1 run of 4 |
| 4 | 1 run of 8 |
| 5 | 7 cards of one colour |
| 6 | 1 run of 9 |
| 7 | 2 sets of 4 |
| 8 | 1 colour run of 4 + 1 set of 3 |
| 9 | 1 set of 5 + 1 set of 3 |
| 10 | 1 set of 5 + 1 colour run of 3 |

- A **set** is cards with the same number, any colours.
- A **run** is cards in consecutive numbers, any colours.
- A **colour run** is a run in which all the cards are the same colour.
- A **phase of one colour** means any numbers, all in the same colour.

## How a hand works

- Each hand you aim at one phase. If you lay down your whole phase you move on
  to the next one; if not, you repeat it in the next hand. Credit comes when
  you lay the phase down, even if another player goes out first.
- The hand ends as soon as a player has laid down their phase and has no cards
  left.
- Players who did not go out score the cards left in their hand as penalty
  points; the player who goes out scores nothing. Cards already laid down do
  not count.

## Penalty points

| Card | Points |
|---|:---:|
| 1 to 9 | 5 |
| 10 to 12 | 10 |
| Action cards | 15 |
| Wild | 20 |

## Other things to know

- Game phases may be played **in any order**. Players need to announce the phase
  they are playing before the round starts.
- The Master box adds **action cards**; see the next section. The app does not
  track them.
- **Card values:** 5 (1–9), 10 (10–12), 15 (Skip) and 25 (Wild).
  Check the player aid in your box. The app only asks for each player's total.

## Action cards

The box has 18 action cards of four kinds, plus 3 blank cards for inventing
your own. Each shows a symbol instead of a number:

| Symbol | Name | Cards |
|---|---|:---:|
| Thumb | **All mine** | 4 |
| Thumb and index finger (two fingers) | **Take two** | 4 |
| Open hand (five fingers) | **Give me five** | 4 |
| Exclamation mark | **Skip** | 6 |

**When they are played.** Ravensburger's own FAQ says action cards are played
**at the end of your turn**, put on the discard pile **instead of discarding a
normal card**. Playing one always ends your turn. So you cannot lay down your
phase on the same turn after playing one, for example after taking a card with
Give me five.

**Thumb: All mine.** If you play it and the hand ends before you finish, you
may **keep any or all the cards in your hand for the next hand**. You decide at the
end of the hand. Those cards still cost you penalty points.

**Two fingers: Take two.** On your next turn you may take **two cards from
the draw pile instead of one**, keep one and discard the other. You still end
the turn with a discard as usual. Sources differ on how long it lasts: one
says only your next turn, another says until the hand ends. Check the rule
book in your box.

**Five fingers: Give me five.** The other players, in turn, show you cards
from their hands until **5 cards in total** are on view. With fewer than six
players, some players show more than one card. You choose one of them and take
it into your hand. The player it came from **draws a replacement from the
draw pile**.

**Exclamation mark: Skip.** You play it in front of an opponent, who misses
their next turn.

## End of the game

The game ends when a player completes phase 10 and goes out. If two or more
players complete it in the same round, the one with the fewest penalty points
over the whole game wins.

## Gamelog tips

- Record for each player the phase aimed at, whether it was completed, and the
  penalty points. The phase list on the side of the board shows the Master
  phases.
- Scores are entered in multiples of 5, and a player who did not complete their
  phase cannot score under 50.
- The **Phases in order** switch is on by default and can only be changed in the
  first round. **Free phase order** lets players choose any phase they have not
  cleared yet; this is a house option, not the standard rule.
- A match is won by the player who has cleared all 10 phases with the lowest
  total. If totals tie, the app prefers the lower score in the last round, and
  if that is also tied the victory is shared.
