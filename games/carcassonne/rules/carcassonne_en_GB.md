# Carcassonne — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Base game, 2–5 players (the app allows up to 6), with
farmers. Three expansions are covered further down: **Inns & Cathedrals**,
**Traders & Builders** and **The Catapult**. The expansion rules follow the
current (revised) editions.*

## Goal

Have the most points when the last tile has been placed and final scoring is
done. Points are scored for **completed** features during the game, and for
**incomplete** features (and fields) at the very end.

| Feature | Completed during the game | Incomplete at game end |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Road | 1 per tile | 1 per tile |
| City | 2 per tile + 2 per coat of arms | 1 per tile + 1 per coat of arms |
| Monastery (cloister) | 9 (itself + 8 neighbours) | 1 + 1 per surrounding tile |
| Field (farmers) | never scored mid-game | 3 per **completed** city touching it |

A tile counts once per feature, however many times the road or city passes
through it. A completed monastery is always 9; it needs all 8 surrounding tiles.

## Setup

1. Put the start tile (dark back) in the middle. Shuffle the other tiles into
   face-down stacks everybody can reach.
2. Each player takes **7 meeple** of one colour; the 8th goes on the score track.
3. Youngest player starts, then play goes clockwise.

## A turn

1. **Place a tile.** Draw exactly one and place it face up next to tiles
   already on the table. Every touching edge must match (road to road, city to
   city, field to field). Monasteries are always in the middle of a tile.
2. **Place a meeple (optional).** One meeple, from your supply, only on the tile
   you just placed, and only on a feature that has **no meeple anywhere on it**,
   even further along the road or city.
3. **Score.** Score every feature your tile completed.

If the tile you drew cannot be placed anywhere legally, return it to the box
and draw another.

## Meeple types

| Role | Placed on | Scored |
|---|---|---|
| Traveler | Road | when completed, or at the end |
| Knight | City | when completed, or at the end |
| Monk | Monastery | when completed, or at the end |
| Farmer | Field (lying down) | **only** at the end; stays there all game |

Scored meeple go back to your supply immediately, except farmers, who never
return. The expansions add the **big follower** (Inns & Cathedrals), and the
**builder** and the **pig** (Traders & Builders); see their sections.

## Completed features

- **Road:** both ends close at a village, city, monastery or other end, or it
  loops back on itself.
- **City:** fully enclosed by walls, with no gaps inside.
- **Monastery:** all 8 surrounding tiles are placed.

## Shared features and ties

Two meeple of different players can end up on the same feature, because you can
place a tile that joins two separately occupied pieces.

- The player with the **most meeple** on the feature scores it in full.
- If several players are **tied** for most meeple, **each of them** scores the
  full points.
- Players with fewer meeple score nothing from that feature.

This applies to fields too: the player with the most farmers on a field scores
the whole field.

## Fields (farmers)

Fields are separated by roads, cities and other dividers, so one tile can touch
several fields. At the end, each field scores **3 points per completed city**
touching it. A city that is still incomplete adds nothing to any field.

## Expansion: Inns & Cathedrals

Adds 18 tiles, a big follower for each player, and the 50 / 100 point tiles
(flip the tile when your score passes 50 or 100, as usual).

- **Big follower:** place it **instead of** a normal follower, on a road, city,
  monastery or field, following the usual placement rules. It counts as **one**
  follower but as **two when deciding who has the majority**. It does not score
  double: a big follower in a monastery scores exactly the same as a normal
  one. It comes back after scoring, like any other follower, except as a farmer,
  where it stays until the end.
- **Inns:** a road with at least one inn beside it scores **2 points per tile**
  when completed, but **0 at the end of the game** if it is still incomplete.
  An inn only affects the road segment next to it, not every road on its tile.
- **Cathedrals:** a city with at least one cathedral scores **3 points per tile
  and 3 per coat of arms** when completed, but **0 at the end of the game** if it
  is incomplete. You may place a cathedral in another player's city, and you may
  do the same with an inn on a road.

## Expansion: Traders & Builders

Adds 24 tiles, a builder and a pig for each player, and 20 trade counters
(9 wine, 6 grain, 5 cloth). Builders and pigs are **not** followers: they never
count towards a majority and cannot be placed without a follower.

**Builder**

- Place it **instead of** a follower, on the tile you just placed, on a road or
  city where **you already have a follower**. Other players' followers may be
  there too. It can never go on a field.
- From then on, whenever you place a tile that **extends** that road or city,
  you take a **double turn**: finish your normal turn (place a follower, score),
  then draw a second tile and play it fully.
- There is no chain reaction: a third tile is never drawn in one turn.
- The builder stays until its road or city is completed and scored, then returns
  to you with your follower. If your last follower leaves that feature, the
  builder comes back too.
- A tile that merely **completes** a feature without extending it (such as an
  abbey) does not give a double turn.

**Pig**

- Place it **instead of** a follower, on the tile you just placed, on a field
  where **you already have a farmer**.
- At the end of the game, if you own the field (you have the majority of
  farmers, or are tied for it), each completed city on it is worth **4 points
  instead of 3**. If you do not score the field, the pig adds nothing.
- The pig stays until the end, unless your last farmer on that field is removed:
  then it returns to you.

**Trade goods**

- Cities with a wine, grain or cloth symbol score as usual when completed. The
  player who **completes** the city takes **one trade counter per symbol**, even
  if they have no knight in it.
- At the end of the game, the player with the **most counters of each type**
  scores **10 points** for it (wine, grain and cloth each count separately).
  Players tied for the most **each** score 10.

## Expansion: The Catapult

Adds 12 tiles with a **fair**, a wooden catapult and a measuring board, and 4
catapult tokens per player (Knock out, Seduction, Target hurling and Catch). A
fair ends a road, as it is a feature of its own.

**Whenever someone draws a fair tile:**

1. They place it and finish their turn as usual.
2. A **catapult round** starts. The player who drew the tile picks one of their
   four tokens and hurls it with the catapult. The other players, clockwise,
   each hurl the **same type** of token. Everybody has **one try**.
3. Everyone takes their tokens back and play continues clockwise.

| Token | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Knock out** | Every follower the token touches goes back to its owner, **including your own**, and a follower knocked into another one removes that one too. Chain reactions count. |
| **Seduction** | The token must come to rest **on the map**; if it slides off, nothing happens. The opponent's follower nearest to it (use the measuring board) is swapped for one of **yours**, from your supply or already on the map. The swapped follower returns to its owner. |
| **Target hurling** | Whoever lands closest to the fair tile that triggered the round scores **5**. Ties each score 5. |
| **Catch** | Hurler and catcher (the player to the hurler's left) take turns until everyone has been both. The hurler puts the measuring board halfway to the catcher and tries to throw past it, and the catcher tries to catch the token. **5 points** to the catcher if they catch it, or if it falls short of the board. **5 points** to the hurler if it passes the board and is not caught, or if the catcher touches it without catching it. |

Followers on the score track are never hit.

## End of the game

The game ends when all tiles are placed. Then:

1. Score all incomplete roads, cities and monasteries as shown in the table.
   (Inns and cathedrals change this; see Inns & Cathedrals.)
2. Score all fields.
3. With Traders & Builders, score the trade counters.
4. Highest total wins. A tie for the highest total is a **shared win**; the
   official rules have no further tie-breaker.

## Gamelog tips

- Home rules: the app does **not** track a board. You enter points as separate
  entries, one per scoring event, and pick the player and the **kind**: City,
  Road, Cloister, Field, Goods or Fair.
- Choosing Cloister presets the score to 9 and caps it at 9.
- **Goods** is locked to **10 points**, which matches each trade-counter
  majority in Traders & Builders (one entry per trade type won). **Fair** is
  locked to **5 points**, which matches each Target hurling or Catch score in
  The Catapult (one entry per player scoring).
- Inns & Cathedrals and the pig have no kind of their own: enter those points
  as Road, City or Field entries.
- Finish the game explicitly when final scoring has been entered; it does not
  end by itself.
- **Tie-break (home rule, differs from the official rules):** if players tie on
  total, the app compares their per-kind totals in the order City, Road,
  Cloister, Field, Goods, Fair. The player ahead on the first kind where they
  differ wins. Only if they are still level on every kind do they share the
  win. Officially a tie is simply shared.
- Enter each tied co-owner's points separately, since each scores in full.
