# Toma6 (6 nimmt!) — quick reference

*A summary for settling disputes at the table, not a replacement for the
official rule book. Basic game, 2–10 players. Pro and Anniversary variants are
not covered.*

## Goal

Collect as **few bullheads** (negative points) as possible. You play several
rounds; when a round leaves someone with the target score or more, the game is
over and the **lowest** total wins. The official target is **66**.

## Cards and bullheads

104 cards numbered 1–104. Each card carries bullheads, which are your penalty
points:

| Card | Bullheads |
|---|:---:|
| Most cards | 1 |
| Ends in 5 (5, 15, 25 ...) | 2 |
| Multiple of 10 (10, 20, 30 ...) | 3 |
| Doubles (11, 22, 33 ...) | 5 |
| 55 (a double ending in 5) | 7 |

## Setup of a round

1. Deal 10 cards to each player.
2. Turn 4 more cards face up in a column. Each starts a **row**.
3. A row never holds more than 5 cards. The rest of the deck is set aside.

## Playing a turn

Everyone secretly picks one card and puts it face down. Once everybody has
chosen, reveal them all. Then place them one at a time, **lowest number first**:

1. **Ascending order:** a card can only go in a row whose last card is lower.
2. **Lowest difference:** of those rows, you must use the one whose last card is
   **closest** to yours. You have no choice.

A round is 10 turns, one per card in hand.

## Taking a row

You pick up cards, put them face down in your own pile, and your played card
becomes the first card of that row in two cases:

- **Full row:** your card would be the 6th in its row. You take the 5 cards that
  are already there.
- **Low card:** your card is lower than the last card of every row. You choose
  **any row** to take (usually the one with the fewest bullheads).

Cards you have taken never go back into your hand.

## End of a round

After the 10th turn, count the bullheads in your pile. Write them down and add
them to your running total. Shuffle everything and deal the next round.

## End of the game

Keep playing rounds until at least one player has reached the target (66 by
default) or more.
The player with the **fewest** points wins. If several players share the lowest
total, they **share the victory**. Reaching the target does not make you the
loser, only the one who ends the game: the lowest total still wins.

The rule book also allows playing to a different score or a fixed number of
rounds if everyone agrees.

## Gamelog tips

- Enter each player's bullhead count at the end of every round.
- The target score is set in the match options (default 66) and in the settings.
  When any player reaches it, the app ends the game and gives the win to the
  lowest total. Players tied on the lowest total share the win, as officially.
- The app does not track the cards themselves, only the round totals.
