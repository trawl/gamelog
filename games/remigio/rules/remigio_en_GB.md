# Remigio — quick reference

*A summary of commonly played Remigio rules for settling disputes at the table.
Many house variants exist (jokers, multipliers, elimination score), so agree on
them first. This sheet follows what the app scores. 2–6 players.*

## Goal

Avoid collecting penalty points. After every round, the players who did not go
out add points for the cards left in their hand. A player who reaches the
**elimination score** (100 by default) is **out**. The **last player left
wins**.

## Setup

- Two 48-card decks plus jokers (100 cards including four jokers). Large groups add
  a third deck.
- Each player is dealt **10 cards**. One card is turned face up to start the
  discard pile and the rest form the stock.

## A turn

1. Draw the top card of the **stock** or of the **discard pile**.
2. Discard one card face up. Play passes on.

## Melds and going out ("cerrar")

- A **meld** is at least **3 cards**: a **set** (same rank) or a **run**
  (consecutive cards of one suit). The ace is low (below the 2) or high (above
  the king).
- A **joker** stands in for any card.
- You **go out** when, at your discard, **all your cards** are in melds. You
  discard your last card face down and everyone then shows their hand.
- Melds are not laid on the table during play: they stay in the hand until
  someone goes out.

## Special hands

| Hand | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Color** | All 10 cards in one suit, no melds needed |
| **Straight flush** (*escalera de color*) | All 10 cards in a single run of one suit |
| **Figures** | All 10 cards of the same rank (needs jokers) |

## Scoring a round

The player who goes out scores **0**. Everyone else counts the cards left in
their hand and **multiplies** the total by the closer's multiplier.

| Card | Points |
|---|---|
| Ace | 1 |
| 2–10 | Face value |
| Jack, Queen, King | 10, 11 and 12 respectively |

| How the round was closed | Multiplier |
|---|:---:|
| Ordinary close, with a joker | 1 |
| No jokers | 2 |
| Color | 3 |
| Figures | 3 |
| Straight flush | 4 |

## Elimination and winning

- Agree the elimination score before round one. 100 is the usual choice.
- A player whose running total **reaches or passes** it is eliminated and does
  not play further rounds.
- The last player still in the game wins.

## Gamelog tips

- Enter each round on the board: for every player still in, type the points
  left in hand. This field accepts 0 to 100 per player.
- To mark who went out, double-click that player's box (or press Space in it).
  Clicking the winner's box again cycles the close multiplier through **1x, 2x,
  3x, 4x**. Their score is 0.
- The app multiplies every other player's entered points by that multiplier
  when the round is saved, so enter raw card points. 
- A player whose total is at or above the elimination score is greyed out and
  skipped when the dealer rotates. The match ends when one active player
  remains.
- The elimination score can only be changed before the first round is
  recorded. Deleting a round reinstates anyone who falls back under it.
- The app has no re-entry or buy-in option, and it does not check hand sizes.
